===SHORT===
Play with music using your bare hands, right inside your own room. Drop a cassette into the radio to pick a song, then step into a mixed reality world that moves with every beat, note and sung word. No controllers needed.
===LONG===
**Handmonic** turns your room into a music visualizer that you play with your bare hands.

**How it works**
- Pick a song by putting its cassette into the radio. Seven original songs come with the app, and you can add your own.
- Choose a world from the bubble menu, then move, pinch and open your hands. Each world follows the beat, the melody and the sung words, so every song plays differently.
- Listen mode follows music playing in the room, from a speaker or a live instrument, through the headset microphone.
- A thumb tap pauses the song and a thumb swipe changes it, in any world.

**A few of the worlds**
- Bubble notes: bubbles rise with the melody while a school of fish swims around you.
- Solar system: the planets circle you, and the lyrics hang overhead as constellations.
- Lightning: rain or snow falls in your room as lightning strikes with the music.
- Flower dance: a garden grows on your floor and leans towards your hand as if it were the sun.
- Also Shadow play, Sound pool, Air strings, Living skin, Earth and water, Finger frame and more.

**Made for your room**
The worlds use your real space: the floor, walls, ceiling and furniture from your room scan, depth so virtual things hide behind real ones, and the passthrough cameras in Finger frame. Everything works with hands only, seated or standing.
