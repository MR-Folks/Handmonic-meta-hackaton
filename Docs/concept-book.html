Music in your hands

A music visualizer for Meta Quest that you play with your bare hands. Turn your palm up, pick one of twelve worlds, and the song lands on your skin, pushes against your fingers and spills into your real room.

Your mouse or finger is your hand. Hold the button down to make a fist or pinch.

How a session goes

One loop, the same in every world, so players learn it once.

01Bring a song

Pick a bundled track, or let the headset listen to music playing on a real speaker in the room.

02Open the wheel

Turn your palm up. Twelve worlds float above it. Point at one and pinch to step inside.

03Play with it

Touch, catch, pinch and push the music. Turn your palm up again at any time to switch worlds without stopping the song.

How your hands feel it

Hand tracking has no controller, so there's no vibration. Each world leans on one of four tricks that make the brain feel touch anyway. The colour follows the world through this page.

Light on your skin

Pulses and glows play across your own hands. You own those hands, so light on them reads as sensation.

Fake resistance

The virtual hand gets nudged, shaken or held back a few millimetres. People report a push that isn't there.

Beat-locked contact

Every touch lands exactly on the beat, so the rhythm seems to arrive through your palm.

Sound at your fingertips

A small sound plays from your hand's position in space each time you touch the music.

The twelve worlds

Each sketch runs on the same beat. Move over one to put your hand in it.

Lyrics in your room

Seven styles made just for the words, each in a different part of your real room. Every world can carry lyrics too, each in its own way: pick one under "Worlds with lyrics" to see it. Slow songs suit Ceiling stars or Word drift; loud ones suit Orbit type, Neon sign or Graffiti.

Lyric styles
Worlds with lyrics

“Glass Room” is an original demo song made for this page. A synth voice sings the melody, and every word lights up as it's sung. Switch styles or worlds while it plays.

Try it with your own song

Plain lyrics: press Tap to sync, then press Space or Next line each time a new line starts. The times are written into the box, so next time press Play with these times. Timed LRC works directly, and word times like <00:12.90> are used when present. Your file and lyrics stay in this browser.

The styles

Getting the words in time

  • Timed lyric files. Each bundled song ships with an LRC file that stamps every line with a time. The enhanced LRC format can time every word too, which these styles need.
  • Use songs you have rights to. Lyrics are copyrighted separately from the recording. For the competition, use your own songs, commissioned tracks, or music whose licence lets you show the words.
  • Music playing in the room has no lyric file. Transcribing a live song from the microphone is unreliable, so lyrics turn on only for bundled tracks.
  • MR Utility Kit knows where things are. It labels walls, floor, ceiling, tables and couches, so each style can find its spot in any room.
  • Keep it readable. Text in a headset has to be large and short-lived: one line at a time, a metre or two away, and only a few words moving at once.

Build notes

Why twelve worlds is realistic: they all share one engine and differ only in what they draw.

  • Unity and the Meta XR All-in-One SDK. The Interaction SDK covers hand tracking, pinch, poke and the palm-up pose that opens the wheel.
  • One music analyser feeds every world. Six frequency bands from AudioSource.GetSpectrumData plus a simple beat detector. Worlds only read those numbers, so adding one is cheap.
  • Each world is its own prefab. The wheel loads one and unloads the last while the song keeps playing.
  • MR Utility Kit for the room. It finds your walls, floor and tables, which Breathing room, Sand table and Sound pool build on.
  • A custom shader on the tracked hand mesh. Living skin, Firefly choir and Light trails all draw on the hand itself.
  • Scope for the deadline. Polish three or four worlds for the judges' first minute and ship the rest as extra worlds.
Sketches are 2D stand-ins for 3D scenes. In the headset, every world is placed in your real room through passthrough. The sample lyrics were written for these sketches.
